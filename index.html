About Me

I'm a hardware engineer with 8+ years of experience designing PCBs and embedded firmware for products that actually ship. My work spans consumer electronics, industrial IoT, and RF systems — from the first schematic to a fully assembled, tested device.

I've taken 20+ boards from concept to production, led hardware and mechanical teams, and love the messy, satisfying middle ground between a datasheet and a working prototype. When I'm not routing traces, I'm writing firmware, tuning antennas, or mentoring junior engineers.
